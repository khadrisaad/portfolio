Lucha Taco is a tiny taquería with a big voice. We cook the way the stands in Mexico City do — fast, loud and with zero shortcuts. Corn is nixtamalized in-house every morning, meats are marinated for 24 hours and every salsa is made from scratch. Grab a stool, squeeze some lime and step into the ring.
